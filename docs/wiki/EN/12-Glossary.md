# 12 – Glossary

## Procurement Terms

**Budget Amount**  
The planned cost or historically paid price for an item or service. It serves as the baseline against which Savings are measured.

**Buyer**  
A professional purchaser within the procurement team. In DataFlow, each buyer has a unique username and owns their RFQs and Value Stream Mapping events.

**Category Manager**  
A buyer responsible for a specific commodity family (category). They may manage a supplier portfolio and negotiate framework agreements.

**Cost Avoidance**  
The economic value generated by blocking or reducing a cost increase requested by a supplier. Unlike Savings, Cost Avoidance does not reduce a cost already being paid — it prevents a future increase. Formula: `(Initial Requested Amount − Negotiated Amount) × Annual Quantity`.

**Derisking**  
The activity of reducing supply chain risk by qualifying alternative or backup suppliers. In DataFlow, the Derisking module manages the potential supplier register.

**Driver (Savings or Cost Avoidance)**  
The negotiation lever that generates the economic value:
- **Price Driver**: reduction in unit price or total contract amount.
- **Payment Terms Driver**: improvement in payment terms (longer payment terms = lower financial cost).

**Full Supply**  
A supply type in which the supplier delivers the finished product (material + processing + any sub-components). Corresponds to the "Full Supply" RFQ type in DataFlow.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**  
A quantitative measure of procurement performance. The main KPIs in DataFlow are Savings, Cost Avoidance, and the number of qualified suppliers.

**OPEX (Operational Expenditure)**  
Recurring operating expense (as opposed to CAPEX, capital expenditure). In DataFlow, a Recurring OPEX event generates economic impacts distributed over time.

**% Realisation**  
The percentage of the theoretical value that is expected to actually be achieved. A saving from a negotiation not yet formalised might have % Realisation = 50%, indicating uncertainty about full achievement. Effective Savings = Theoretical Savings × (% Realisation / 100).

**PO (Purchase Order)**  
The formal document with which the buying company orders goods or services from a supplier, specifying quantity, price, and terms.

**RFQ (Request for Quotation)**  
A document sent to one or more suppliers requesting a quote for the supply of goods or services. In DataFlow, an RFQ is a record containing a price grid, attachments, and supplier management tools.

**Savings**  
A cost reduction relative to the budget or historical cost. Basic formula: `(Budget Amount − Negotiated Amount) × Annual Quantity`.

**Annual Spend**  
Total annual expenditure with a supplier, used as the calculation base for savings derived from payment term improvements.

**SQDC**  
A multi-criteria evaluation framework: Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost. Used to compare suppliers in a structured and objective way.

**Subcontracting**  
A supply type in which the raw material is provided by the buying company and the supplier performs only the processing or manufacturing work. Corresponds to the "Subcontracting" RFQ type in DataFlow.

## DataFlow Application Terms

**Carry-over**  
Economic value (Savings or Cost Avoidance) generated by events in the current or previous year that manifests in the following year. Visible as an additional KPI when using the year filter in the KPI Dashboard. Useful for budget projections.

**Economic Competence (Period)**  
The month in which an economic impact is recognised from an accounting perspective. In DataFlow, Savings and Cost Avoidance KPIs are filtered by competence period, not by the date the event was entered.

**Value Stream Mapping Event**  
A record of a negotiation or Derisking activity in the Value Stream Mapping module. Each event generates one or more monthly impacts representing the distribution of the economic value over time.

**Monthly Impact**  
A record derived from a Value Stream Mapping event that represents the theoretical and effective economic value attributed to a single month. It is the minimum granularity used by the KPIs.

**Read-Only Mode**  
The automatic behaviour of DataFlow when a user opens a record (RFQ, event, supplier) that belongs to another user. All editing fields are disabled; full read access is preserved.

**Username**  
The unique identifier for each buyer, automatically generated at first launch in the format `firstname.lastname`. It cannot be changed by the user.

**First-Month Pro-Rata**  
A coefficient applied to the first month of distribution for a Recurring OPEX event. It accounts for the fact that the event occurred on a specific day of the month, not on the first day. Formula: `(30 − day + 1) / 30`.

**Value Stream Mapping**  
In the context of DataFlow, this refers to the procurement value-tracking module that records Savings, Cost Avoidance, and Derisking activities. The name is inspired by the Lean manufacturing methodology of the same name, adapted to a procurement context.

**WAL (Write-Ahead Log)**  
An operating mode of the SQLite database engine that allows multiple users to read simultaneously while one user writes. It ensures data consistency in shared multi-user environments without requiring a dedicated database server.
